# The Invisible Chain

**The Invisible Human Cost of Global Logistics**: a research site on why physical labor is systematically undervalued in global supply chains, and how poverty chains keep workers in cycles of inequality.

Originally built on Google Sites (`sites.google.com/view/the-invisible-chain`), rebuilt here as a plain static site that can be hosted free on GitHub Pages.

## Pages

| File | Page |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Home: central question, stance, about the author |
| `background.html` | The Background: the Smile Curve, key terms, history |
| `data.html` | The Data: evidence, Maya Aktar's testimony, AI data labor |
| `solutions.html` | Sociology & Policy Solutions |
| `conclusion.html` | Conclusion |
| `works-cited.html` | Works Cited (APA 7) |

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. In the repo, go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
3. The site appears at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/` within a minute or two.

## What changed from the Google Sites version

**Design and usability**
- Responsive layout with a sticky menu that collapses on phones, automatic dark mode, and previous/next links at the bottom of every page.
- Key terms shown as cards; policy proposals grouped as numbered cards; the testimony set as a pull quote.
- Descriptive alt text on every image, a skip-to-content link, page descriptions for search and link previews.
- YouTube videos embedded in privacy-enhanced mode and loaded lazily.

**Citation fixes** (in-text citations now match the Works Cited)
- *The Background*: "Rungi and Del Prete (2017)" → **(2018)**, the publication year in the reference.
- *The Data*: the *Canadian Journal of Economics* Smile Curve study is **Baldwin and Ito (2021)**, not Rungi and Del Prete (2021).
- *The Data*: "Casilli and Posada (2024)… from the International Labour Organization" → **Casilli et al. (2025)**, the four-country data-work study in the Works Cited. If you meant a separate Casilli & Posada ILO source, add it to the Works Cited and restore the original wording.
- Works Cited converted from a mix of AMA and MLA to consistent **APA 7**, alphabetized, with clickable DOIs; the UN News piece is now listed under Aktar (2022) to match the in-text citation.

**Text edits**
- Fixed small typos ("Whats" → "What's", stray spacing around "invisible"), removed a duplicated heading on The Data page, and turned "What's the Solution?" into a link to the next page.
- Linked the three organizations on the Conclusion page.
